PyFoot is a 1v1 arcade football game built with Pygame where you face off against an AI opponent in a 60-second match.

You control a car on the left side of the field and try to kick a fireball into the opponent's goal. The arena features angled corner pieces that give the ball unpredictable bounces, and powerups periodically spawn on the field — grab them to temporarily boost your speed, jump height, or boost duration.
